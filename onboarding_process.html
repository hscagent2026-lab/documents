Purpose

This document describes how new employees join JugnuParatha Cloth Company, from offer acceptance until confirmation.

Before Day One

The offer letter, appointment letter and joining checklist are emailed at least 7 days before the joining date.

New joiners submit identity proof, address proof, educational certificates, previous employment relieving letter and two passport-size photographs.

Day One

Report to the HR desk at the Ludhiana head office at 9:30 AM.

Collect the ID card, laptop and access card, and attend the welcome session.

Induction contact: Kavita Rao (HR Business Partner).

Probation Period

All new employees serve a probation period of 6 months from the date of joining.

Confirmation is based on the manager's probation review and attendance record.

Onboarding Buddy

Every new joiner is assigned an onboarding buddy for the first 30 days.
