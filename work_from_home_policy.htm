Work From Home Policy

Company Name: JugnuParatha Cloth Company

Document: Work From Home Policy

Last Updated: 10 February 2026

Last Modified By: Anjali Sharma, HR Operations

Version Number: 1.0

Eligibility

Office-based employees who have completed probation are eligible. Factory floor and warehouse roles are not eligible.

Allowed Days

Eligible employees may work from home up to 2 days per week.

Requests must be raised in the HR portal at least 1 working day in advance.

Allowance

Employees using work from home receive an internet allowance of INR 800 per month.

Expectations

Employees must be reachable on phone and chat during core hours.
