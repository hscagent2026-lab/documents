Purpose

This policy sets out how open positions are approved, advertised and filled.

Hiring Process

Positions are approved by the department head and HR before posting.

Candidates go through 3 interview rounds: HR screening, technical/functional interview and final interview with the department head.

Employee Referral Programme

Employees who refer a candidate who is hired receive a referral bonus of INR 15,000.

The bonus is paid after the referred employee completes 90 days of service.

Equal Opportunity

JugnuParatha Cloth Company does not discriminate on the basis of gender, religion, caste, age or disability.
