Attendance and Working Hours Policy

Company Name: JugnuParatha Cloth Company

Document: Attendance and Working Hours Policy

Last Updated: 05 February 2026

Last Modified By: Anjali Sharma, HR Operations

Version Number: 1.0

Working Days

The office works Monday to Saturday; the second and fourth Saturdays are holidays.

Working Hours

Each working day is 9 hours including a 45-minute lunch break.

All employees must be present during core hours of 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

Late Arrival

Arriving after core start time is recorded as a late mark.

Up to 3 late marks per month are allowed; each further late mark deducts half a day of leave.

Attendance Recording

Attendance is recorded with the biometric system at the factory and office gates.
